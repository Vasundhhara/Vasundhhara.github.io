Enterprise AI Product Line — Iris

The AI product line of a multi-tenant enterprise engagement platform serving 250+ customers — 10+ shipped AI capabilities on a provider-agnostic LLM foundation, cutting agent response drafting 4×.

▶ Watch product demo

The problem

Enterprise support and social engagement teams handle 100M+ conversations a year. Drafting responses, triaging tickets, and understanding sentiment at that volume was entirely manual — and any AI solution had to work across tenants with strict cost, audit, and rollout controls.

What I built

  • Post-acquisition strategy & roadmap: contributed to modernizing a multi-tenant enterprise engagement platform serving 250+ customers across 27 social channels and processing 100M+ conversations annually — delivering the MVP in 3 months.
  • Provider-agnostic LLM infrastructure supporting OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and Llama models with automated failover, observability, audit trails, and token-cost tracking via Langfuse and Prometheus.
  • 10+ AI capabilities: summarization, sentiment analysis, translation, entity extraction, AI-assisted response generation, and intelligent ticket prioritization — orchestrated through Kafka, FastAPI, MongoDB, and asynchronous batch processing.
  • Per-tenant governance: feature-level rollout, per-tenant model controls, and token-cost budgets that made AI safe to sell to enterprise buyers.
  • Inference & delivery layer: containerized FastAPI services on Kubernetes with CI/CD automation and GPU-based inference.

Outcomes

  • Agent response drafting cut from 3–4 minutes to ~1 minute (4× faster).
  • Product metrics (adoption, AI usage, cost-per-interaction) instrumented in Grafana + Langfuse and reviewed weekly to steer the roadmap.
