RAG-Based AI Hiring Assistant

Hushh AI · 2023
RAGOpenAILangChainHugging FacePDF Parsing

An end-to-end AI assistant that automates candidate screening — parsing resumes, evaluating candidates against role requirements with retrieval-augmented generation, and ranking them for recruiters.

How it works

  • Ingestion: PDF parsing pipelines extract structured candidate profiles from unstructured resumes (Pandas, PDF parsing techniques).
  • Evaluation: RAG grounds LLM judgments in the actual job description and candidate documents, reducing hallucinated assessments (OpenAI, LangChain, Hugging Face).
  • Screening: automated candidate scoring and shortlisting, turning hours of manual review into minutes.

Why it matters

This was one of the first internal proofs that LLM + retrieval pipelines could replace a real, repetitive business workflow end to end — shaping how the team approached later AI products.
