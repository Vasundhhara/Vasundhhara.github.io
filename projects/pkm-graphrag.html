AI Personal Knowledge Management System

Hushh AI · 2023 — 2025
GraphRAGLightRAGNeo4jLangChainLangGraphGraphQL

An AI-driven personalized chatbot over a user's structured and unstructured personal data — with three retrieval architectures designed, built, and benchmarked to find the best contextual search approach.

The problem

Personal data lives across many sources and formats. A personalization engine needs retrieval that understands relationships (people, events, preferences) — not just keyword or embedding similarity.

Retrieval architectures explored

  • LLM-generated GraphQL queries — the model writes structured queries against user data. Stack: GraphQL, LangChain, LLaMA models, Hugging Face, Ollama, FastAPI.
  • LightRAG — implemented from the research paper for lightweight graph-augmented retrieval. Stack: FastAPI.
  • GraphRAG over Neo4j — knowledge-graph construction and graph-traversal-based retrieval for relationship-aware answers. Stack: FastAPI, Neo4j, Cypher.

Platform work

  • Owned the full lifecycle: system architecture, testing, and deployment.
  • Designed a centralized PostgreSQL data architecture as the shared backend across products — multi-source ingestion, multi-channel retrieval.
  • Led deployment strategy across Vertex AI, GCP, Docker, Hugging Face, and self-hosted GPUs, optimizing inference cost, latency, and scalability.
